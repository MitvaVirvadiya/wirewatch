# 001 — Naming (D10: meaningful, real-startup quality)

Working name: **Wirewatch** — "we watch the wires" (feeds, webhooks, files) between
you and your financial providers. Meaningful to the domain, memorable, two syllables.

## Shortlist (founder may veto/rerank)

| Name | Meaning | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| **Wirewatch** (default) | Watching the wires/feeds | Operational, trustworthy |
| Feedpulse | The heartbeat of your data feeds | Product-y, descriptive |
| Tapewatch | Ticker tape → watching market data flow | Finance heritage, clever |
| Signalpost | Where provider signals arrive and get checked | Infra-ish |
| Beaconwire | Early warning on the wire | Alert-focused |

## Naming criteria applied

- Explains itself to a fintech engineer in <3 seconds
- Not a generic "-ify/-ly" placeholder
- Survives the later absorption of Direction 1 (recon) & 3 (ledger) —
  all five candidates still make sense for "exception ops"
- Domain/handle availability: TODO before any public demo (Phase 2 task)

**Status:** ✅ `accepted` (2026-10-14, founder confirmed "good name"). Package scope `@wirewatch/*`. Domain/handle availability check remains a Phase 2 to-do before any public demo.
